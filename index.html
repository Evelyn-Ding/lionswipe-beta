What LionSwipe Does

LionSwipe is used by Columbia students who want to get more out of their meal plan. It displays live dining hall menus for you to track your meal swipes, as well as spending on eating out and grocery shopping—so you never have to guess.

The Problem We're Solving

It's easy to lose track of how many meal swipes you have left or how much money you've been spending on eating out. LionSwipe offers live dining data and a comprehensive dashboard with your meal swipe and spending information all in one place, so you can save time and energy when deciding what to eat.

What Info We Ask For

All you have to input is your meal plan for the semester, and you can add spending goals if you want to budget. Then, you just log dining hall meal swipes and off-campus spending as you go. You can use LionSwipe as a guest with no log-in required (your data stays in your browser). Sign up any time for an account to make saving and managing your data easier.

Our Story

LionSwipe is built by Evelyn and Zoey, who are sophomores studying CS at Columbia. The idea was born in Summer 2026, and we are constantly working on improving it for users. Feel free to leave feedback at this form or fill out our dining survey :)

Finding Your Meal Swipes on SSOL

  1. Go to ssol.columbia.edu and log in.
  2. Click the ☰ Dashboard button, then go to Campus Services → Dining → View Cardholder Transactions.
Why LionSwipe keeps its own count instead of just showing SSOL:
  • Weekly plans reset every Sunday with a fresh balance — nothing carries between weeks or terms. Term-based plans instead carry unused Fall swipes into Spring, but those carried-over swipes aren't usable (or visible on SSOL at all) until you've used up your entire Spring allowance first — and everything expires at the end of the academic year.
  • That means once rollover kicks in, SSOL only shows your current term's balance, not your real combined total — so its number and your actual total quietly drift apart. That's exactly the gap LionSwipe tracks for you — use "Correct Swipes Left" here whenever the two disagree. :)
